About Difathar Soccer Academy

Difathar Soccer Academy is a youth-focused soccer development organization committed to identifying, nurturing, and developing soccer talent at the grassroots level. The academy provides structured training programs designed to build skilled, disciplined, and confident players who can compete at local, national, and international levels.

Founded with a vision to empower young people through soccer, the academy uses the sport as a tool for personal development, education, and positive social change. Difathar Soccer Academy provides professional coaching, mentorship and life skills training while promoting integrity, respect, hard work and resilience.
